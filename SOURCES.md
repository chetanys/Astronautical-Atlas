## Scope and notes

- Links are grouped by the workbook sheet/topic in which they appeared.
- Descriptive labels are copied from nearby spreadsheet cells; they are context, not independent verification of the linked content.
- Some links are secondary sources, archived copies, or may need checking before being cited as scientific evidence.
- URL strings are deduplicated; different URLs that lead to the same resource are retained separately.
